# Current limitations

- **Prepared presentation:** `/demo` reuses one reviewed Meta-generated adventure. The experimental `/preview` can fail during content validation and is not a reliable live-generation presentation.
- **Narrative boundaries:** generation uses a bounded three-decision structure with 27 A/B/timeout routes and six ending states. Automated contract checks do not guarantee good prose or continuity in a new manuscript. Known prepared-story wording and repetition are retained to preserve its committed bytes.
- **Human outcomes:** the experience aims to make a short story memorable and responsive to declared preferences. It has not established therapeutic benefit, psychological inference, universal uniqueness, or a ten-to-twelve-minute reading duration for every reader.
- **Timing and recovery:** deadlines and choices are server-authoritative. Existing automated checks cover expiry and reconnection. Browser emulation is not a physical-phone or every-network certification.
- **Transparency:** the source includes the prepared fictional branches and reveal. Ordinary play withholds the envelope until the end, but open source is not a secrecy boundary against source inspection.
- **Scope:** no multiplayer, voice input, app-store package, long asynchronous adventure or automatic AI playtester is included in this entry.
- **Provider access:** enabling preparation requires a private server-side Meta key and may incur charges. The prepared entry works without it. Provider quotas, pricing and model availability can change; this repository does not guarantee current access.
- **Data:** production stores journeys and diagnostic information. None of those private stores, messages, owner tokens or provider receipts are included here. Operators must review retention and hosting configuration for their own deployment.
